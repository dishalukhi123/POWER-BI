Section 01Project Overview

📌 What This Dashboard Does

This report was built in Power BI Desktop on top of the classic Sample Superstore dataset. It answers the core questions any retail sales team asks: which category sells best, which region is most profitable, where our top cities are, and how discounting is affecting margins.

Everything on the page is cross-filterable — click any bar, slice, or map point and the rest of the dashboard updates instantly.

Why It MattersA single glance at the KPI cards, category breakdown, and regional map gives decision-makers a real-time pulse on sales health without digging through spreadsheets.

Section 07Key Insights

  • Sales are fairly balanced across Technology, Office Supplies, and Furniture — each contributing close to a third of total sales.
  • The Central region stands out clearly in the Profit by Region view.
  • A small number of cities generate a disproportionately large share of total sales, visible as outliers in the scatter plot.
  • Average discount sits around 24% — worth cross-checking against the profit trend to spot margin risk.
Next StepAdd a discount-vs-profit scatter or trend line to quantify exactly where high discounting starts eroding margin.
